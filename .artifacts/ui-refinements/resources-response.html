A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear next steps.

Who are Accent’s programs for?+

Students, graduates and career switchers, colleges, and companies investing in their teams.

Which offerings are available now?+

Master classes are the current live offering. Planned offerings are marked Opening soon. Contact Accent to discuss the listed college and corporate services.

Can college and corporate programs be tailored?+

Yes. Share your students’ or team’s priorities in a campus conversation or free needs call to discuss the scope.

How do I confirm a date or place?+

Use the registration or enquiry form for your chosen offering. Your request is sent to Accent, who will confirm dates and availability with you.
